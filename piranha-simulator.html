PIRANHASIMULATOR

You are a swarm of piranhas loose in the Amazon. Eat smaller fish to grow the swarm. Big fish bite back until you are big enough to take them. A giant anaconda hides in the lagoon at the centre of the map.

  • HOLD & DRAGThe swarm swims toward your thumb.
  • DOUBLE-TAPScatter so attackers lose track of you, then regroup where your thumb is. Short cooldown.
  • RED SKULLToo big to eat. It bites once per tier, then swims off. If it is far bigger than you, it hunts you to death.
  • HUNGERFrom LV 2 the swarm starves unless it keeps eating. The bigger it is, the faster it starves.
  • KEYBOARDWASD or arrows to swim, Space to scatter, P to pause.

Every game carves a new river.
